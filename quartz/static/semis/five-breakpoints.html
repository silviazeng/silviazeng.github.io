写法说明

大部分半导体简史是发明年表：谁在哪一年做出了什么。那种写法读完记不住，因为发明本身不解释结构。

这篇换一个问法：在每个阶段，钱和定价权在谁手上，以及它为什么会移动。 按这个标准，八十年里只有五个真正的断点。

断点五：超大规模厂商自研（2015–今）

动机按重要性排：

  1. 把毛利内化。 英伟达数据中心毛利率长期在 70% 以上，超大规模厂商每买一块 GPU 都在向它输送毛利。
  2. 工作负载确定性。 自家推理负载高度同质、规模巨大，可以砍掉通用性换能效比。
  3. 供应安全。 摆脱单一供应商的分配权。
  4. 规模足够摊薄。 只有当你自己能消化几十万到上百万颗时，几亿美元的设计成本才算得过来。

How this is written

Most short histories of semiconductors are invention timelines: who made what, when. They're hard to retain, because inventions don't explain structure.

This one asks a different question: at each stage, who held the money and the pricing power, and why did it move? By that standard, eighty years contain only five real breakpoints.

Breakpoint 5: Hyperscalers go in-house (2015–now)

Motivations, in order of weight:

  1. Internalize the margin. NVIDIA's data center gross margin has run above 70%; every GPU a hyperscaler buys transfers margin to NVIDIA.
  2. Workload certainty. In-house inference is homogeneous and enormous, so you can trade generality for perf-per-watt.
  3. Supply security. Escape a single vendor's allocation power.
  4. Enough scale to amortize. Hundreds of millions in design cost only pencils out if you can absorb hundreds of thousands to millions of units yourself.
