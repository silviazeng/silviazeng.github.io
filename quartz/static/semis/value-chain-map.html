Named on the Entity List. Huawei in 2019, SMIC in 2020, Sophgo in 2024, and over 140 entities added at once in December 2024. Exports of controlled items to a listed party need a licence, normally under a policy of presumptive denial.

外国直接产品规则（FDP）。一件产品哪怕百分之百在台湾制造、由台湾公司拥有，只要它是用美国的技术、软件或设备「直接生产」出来的，美国就主张管辖权——不论它在哪造、由谁造。这才是台积电受美国法律约束的原因，而不是它有多少美国客户。2020 年针对华为启用，2022 年扩展到先进计算，2024 年 12 月又扩展到半导体设备本身。One instrument of the same US export-control system (the EAR, run by BIS) — five tools each bite one layer, and this one governs whether a foreign-made product still falls under US law.

The Foreign Direct Product Rule. Even an item made entirely in Taiwan by a Taiwanese company falls under US jurisdiction if it is the direct product of American technology or equipment. That, not its customer list, is why TSMC is bound by US law. 与and 台湾对外投资审查Taiwan outbound investment review《产业创新条例》第 22 条修正（2025 年 4 月通过）。对外投资特定技术需事前核准，主管机关可以国家安全为由否决或附条件核准。所谓「N-1 原则」（海外制程落后本土一代）从来不是成文禁令，而是通过这道审查行使的裁量性规范，2025 年 1 月起持续放宽。Article 22 of the Industrial Innovation Act, amended April 2025. 两种模式：IDM 与 fabless + 代工Two models: IDM, or fabless plus foundry
这一层最容易被忽略的分野不是国别，而是是否自建产线。IDM 自行设计并自行制造（下方存储一排全部如此，模拟与功率中亦占多数），fabless 只做设计，将硅片交由代工厂——两条路的成本结构、护城河和风险完全不同，逐条对照IDM 与 fabless：不是谁更先进，是什么时候哪种划算
定义：IDM 自行设计、自建产线制造，多数亦自行封测；fabless 只做设计，制造交由代工厂、封测交由 OSAT
谁是 IDM：存储清一色（三星、海力士、美光、铠侠、长鑫、长江存储）；模拟、功率、车规 MCU 多数是（TI、英飞凌、意法、恩智浦、瑞萨）；先进逻辑仅余英特尔仍在坚持
IDM 的优势：工艺与产品协同调校——存储与功率器件的性能本就取决于工艺，外包即让渡核心；产能自主调度；旧产线折旧摊销完毕后成本极低
IDM 的代价：一座先进厂几百亿美元，产能空转就是亏损；工艺一旦落后，产品跟着落后，英特尔 10nm 就是教科书案例
什么时候 IDM 赢：工艺即产品、不必追前沿节点、一颗料号卖十年——存储、模拟、功率、车规
什么时候 fabless 赢：要前沿节点、迭代快、股东要求资本回报——AI、手机、低功耗无线
分水岭：1987 年台积电发明纯代工，把「要不要建厂」从战略问题变成采购问题，fabless 才成为可行路径
反方向：图里那条琥珀虚线（云厂商自研芯片）是同一逻辑的反向——系统厂向上游延伸，同样在把链条收拢回自己手中
对低功耗这条赛道：Nordic、乐鑫、泰凌微全是 fabless，TI 是 IDM。TI 收购 SiLabs 之后，赛道里第一次出现以自有 300mm 产线的成本参与竞争的对手
一句话：IDM 押的是产线利用率，fabless 押的是设计迭代速度。。The divide most easily missed on this layer is not nationality but who owns fabs. An IDM designs and manufactures (the whole memory row below, and most of the analog and power names); a fabless company designs and hands the silicon to a foundry — two different cost structures, moats and risks, line by lineIDM vs fabless: not which is more advanced, but when each pays
Definition: an IDM designs and manufactures in its own fabs, usually packaging and testing too; a fabless firm designs only, buying wafers from a foundry and assembly from an OSAT
Who is an IDM: every memory maker here (Samsung, SK hynix, Micron, Kioxia, CXMT, YMTC); most analog, power and automotive-MCU names (TI, Infineon, ST, NXP, Renesas); in leading-edge logic only Intel still holds the line
What it buys: process and product tuned together — in memory and power devices the performance lives in the process, so outsourcing means handing over the core; capacity you allocate yourself; and once a line is depreciated, costs almost nobody can match
What it costs: tens of billions per leading-edge fab, and idle capacity is a straight loss; when the process falls behind, the products fall behind with it — Intel at 10nm is the textbook case
When IDM wins: the process is the product, the node need not be the newest, and a part sells for a decade — memory, analog, power, automotive
When fabless wins: leading-edge nodes, fast iteration, shareholders wanting returns on capital — AI, phones, low-power wireless
The hinge: TSMC's pure-play model in 1987 turned "should we build a fab" from a strategy question into a purchasing one, which is what made fabless viable at all
The other direction: the amber dashed loop above (hyperscalers designing their own chips) is the same move in reverse — a systems company growing upstream, sewing the chain back together
For the low-power track: Nordic, Espressif and Telink are all fabless; TI is an IDM. Once TI closes on Silicon Labs, the track has, for the first time, a rival competing on the cost of its own 300mm fabs
In one line: an IDM bets on fab utilization, a fabless company bets on design cadence..

07低功耗无线连接 SoCLow-Power Wireless SoC

55/40/22nm，不排 CoWoS 的队，不受先进节点资本门槛约束。竞争维度是功耗、协议覆盖、认证、SDK 易用性和成本——不是算力。55/40/22nm, no CoWoS queue, none of the leading-edge capital barrier. The competitive axes are power, protocol coverage, certification, SDK usability and cost — not compute.

物联网芯片和 AI 芯片的逻辑不同IoT chips run on a different logic from AI chips
单价几毛到几美元，年出货以亿颗计，而毛利率经常高于 AI 时代的很多明星。差别不在程度，在方向——逐条对照不是程度差别，是方向相反：
制程：AI 越先进越好 → 物联网用 55/40/22nm，先进节点漏电大，而电池要撑十年
瓶颈：AI 抢 CoWoS 产能 → 物联网不排队，瓶颈在 design win
客户：AI 几十家 → 物联网几千家（TI 十万家）
卖点：AI 拼算力 → 物联网拼功耗、协议覆盖、认证、SDK
毛利来源：AI 是技术代差 → 物联网是转换成本
格局：AI 赢家通吃 → 物联网场景碎片，没人通吃
门槛：AI 是资本 → 物联网是时间（认证与协议栈的累积）
生命周期：AI 一年一代 → 物联网一颗料号卖十年，客户要 5–10 年供货承诺
价格：AI 缺货涨价 → 物联网合同里写着逐年降价
软件：CUDA 是「用了就走不了」→ SDK 是「好用才会来」
一句话：AI 芯片卖能力上限，物联网芯片卖「在约束下刚好够用、且一直供得上」。。Unit prices run cents to a few dollars, annual shipments in the hundreds of millions — and gross margins often beat the AI era's stars. The difference is one of direction, not degree — line by lineNot a difference of degree — the arrows point opposite ways:
Node: AI wants the newest → IoT runs 55/40/22nm; leading-edge leaks, and the battery must last ten years
Bottleneck: AI queues for CoWoS → IoT has no queue; the constraint is the design win
Customers: AI has dozens → IoT has thousands (TI has 100,000)
What sells: AI sells compute → IoT sells power draw, protocol coverage, certification, SDK
Source of margin: AI is a technology gap → IoT is switching cost
Structure: AI is winner-take-most → IoT is too fragmented for anyone to take all
Barrier: AI is capital → IoT is time, as certifications and stacks accumulate
Lifecycle: AI turns over yearly → an IoT part number sells for a decade, on 5–10 year supply commitments
Pricing: AI raises prices on shortage → IoT writes annual price-downs into the contract
Software: CUDA locks you in → an SDK has to earn you in
In one line: AI chips sell a ceiling of capability; IoT chips sell being exactly enough under constraint, and still shipping in ten years..

护城河在硅之外 / Moat Is Not in Silicon

协议认证SIG / Zigbee / Thread / MatterSIG / Zigbee / Thread / Matter认证。Bluetooth SIG、Zigbee、Thread、Matter、Z-Wave，加各国无线电型号核准（FCC/CE/SRRC/MIC）。这是合规资产，不产生技术优势，但要时间和钱堆，且每出一个新协议版本就要重来。Certification — Bluetooth SIG, Zigbee, Thread, Matter, Z-Wave, plus radio type approvals (FCC/CE/SRRC/MIC). A compliance asset: no technical edge, but it takes time and money to build, and every new protocol version resets the clock.SDK 惯性真正的转换成本the real switching costSDK 与固件惯性。客户写完固件、过完认证、量产铺货后，换一颗便宜 5 毛的芯片意味着重写、重认证、重跑可靠性。这行的 50% 毛利率不是垄断租金，是转换成本租金。SDK and firmware inertia. Once a customer has written firmware, passed certification and shipped, switching to a chip 5 cents cheaper means rewriting, recertifying, requalifying. The 50% gross margins here are switching-cost rent, not monopoly rent.生态标准方Apple / Google / Amazon / SamsungApple / Google / Amazon / Samsung定价权在这里。Matter、Find My、各自的 Home 生态决定「什么算能用的设备」。芯片厂扮演的是「合规通过率 + SDK 易用性」的供应商。Pricing power lives here. Matter, Find My and the walled Home ecosystems decide what counts as a working device. The chip vendor's role is supplier of compliance pass-rates and SDK usability.

08认证与生态Certification & Ecosystems

芯片厂控制不了的一道门。决定「什么算一台能用的设备」的不是芯片本身，是标准组织与核准机构。A gate no chip vendor controls. What counts as a working device is decided not by the chip but by standards bodies and radio regulators.

这道门决定了定价权落在谁手上This gate decides where pricing power lands
一颗 SoC 通过认证、拿到型号核准、进入某个生态，才成为终端厂商能用的东西。换一颗便宜五分钱的芯片意味着重写、重认证、重核准——这份成本落在客户身上，议价的位置也就留在了标准方和终端厂商那边。A SoC becomes usable to a device maker only once it has passed certification, cleared radio approval and entered an ecosystem. Switching to a chip five cents cheaper means rewriting, recertifying and requalifying — that cost falls on the customer, which is what leaves the bargaining position with the standard-setters and the device makers.
