**Stet Money** is an iOS app built by an individual developer. If something is
broken, confusing, or missing, write to **stet.money.app@gmail.com**. Replies
usually come within one business day.

When you write, it helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and
the app version (Settings inside the app shows it at the bottom). If a receipt
was read incorrectly, say which merchant and what the app got wrong.

---

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. The sign-in screen has "Continue without an account" at the bottom, and
every feature works without one. There is no server, so signing in does not
upload anything; it only keeps a credential on your device so the app can
recognise you on that phone.

**Where is my data?**
On your phone. Receipts, quiz answers, and your archetype are stored locally.
The only thing that leaves the device is a receipt photo or the text read from
it, sent to a cloud service to be turned into structured text, plus anonymous
crash and usage reports. The [Privacy Policy](/legal/stet-privacy-policy)
lists every destination by name.

**How do I delete my account?**
While signed in, open Settings (the gear on the Home screen) and scroll to
"Delete account". It removes the stored credentials from your device. Your
receipts and your reading are kept, because they are not tied to the account.

**How do I delete my receipts?**
Open a receipt from the Spending list and delete it there. Deleting the app
removes everything at once, since nothing is stored anywhere else.

**A receipt was read wrong.**
Every field is editable after a scan, so you can correct it before or after
saving. Misreads usually come from clutter in the photo: another receipt in
frame, packaging, a patterned table. One receipt, laid flat, filling the
frame, reads best. If one keeps failing, write and describe it, including the
merchant and which line came out wrong. Please do not email the receipt
itself; your receipts are yours, and there is nowhere on our side to put
them.

**Why does Home show "…" for income?**
Stet Money has no bank connection. It only knows what you add, so it reports
spending and leaves income blank rather than inventing a number.

**Why did my archetype change?**
The first five questions give a starting point. After that your real receipts
move it, and around day 14 it settles. A change means the pattern in your
spending moved, not that the earlier reading was wrong.

**How do I retake the quiz?**
There is no in-app control for this yet. Deleting and reinstalling the app
starts onboarding again, but it also deletes your receipts.

## Reporting a problem

Email **stet.money.app@gmail.com**. Please say what you expected, what
happened instead, and the steps that led there. Screenshots help. Bugs that
lose or misread data are treated first.
