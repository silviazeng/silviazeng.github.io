Recently I tried to explain to family why one company in Taiwan prints most of the world's advanced chips, and found I couldn't do it without a whiteboard.

So I wrote the whiteboard down. Most chip-industry histories are invention timelines — who built what in which year — and they don't stick, because inventions don't explain structure. **The question that makes the industry legible is who holds the pricing power, and why it moves.** Asked that way, eighty years collapse into five breakpoints, and the last one is happening right now.
